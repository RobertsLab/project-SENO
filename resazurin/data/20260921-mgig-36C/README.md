`resazurin/data/20260921-mgig-36C`

### DESCRIPTION

Adult _M.gigas_ (Pacific oyster) oysters were previously subjected to an "El Nino" treatment (parameters/duration need info from Steven Roberts) and then held at 10<sup>o</sup>C (need duration info fom Steven Roberts) in artificial seawater (Instant Ocean). Control (n = 20) and "El Nino" oysters (n = 20) were transferred to 12oz plastic cups containing 125mL of resazurin working solution and subjected to a four hour heat stress at 36<sup>o</sup> in an floor-standing incubator.

Every hour, 200uL of resazurin was transferred from each cup to a well in a clear, 96-well plate. The plates were stored O/N at 4<sup>o</sup>C, covered in foil. The following morning, resazurin fluorescence was measured using a Synergy HTX (Agilent).
